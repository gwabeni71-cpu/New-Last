Who we are

BOLO (Music, TV, Movies, News and Dream Hub) is published by Kutullo Angel Media. Contact: [add your contact email here].

What we do not do

BOLO has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We do not sell your data. BOLO shows one clearly labelled "Ad" strip with sponsored links; nothing is ever placed over a video.

Data stored on your device

Favourites, recently played items, watch history, streaks, settings and cached content are stored in your browser's local storage on your device only. You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data for BOLO in your browser or phone settings.

Anonymous usage counter

The Music section uses Google Firebase to show how many people are online. It stores a random anonymous identifier (not linked to you) while the app is open and for the current day's visitor count.

Third-party services

YouTube API Services. BOLO uses YouTube API Services and the embedded YouTube player. By using BOLO you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube may set cookies or collect data when you play videos.

Sponsored links. When you tap a sponsored link you leave BOLO and the advertiser or ad network (Monetag, Adsterra) handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Movie information comes from TMDB. News is loaded from our public blogs. BOLO Insider sends the question you type to an AI service (Groq) through our secure server to produce an answer. AI answers may be inaccurate; do not enter personal information.

Children

BOLO is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Content and rights

Videos are streamed from YouTube and remain the property of their owners. BOLO does not host or download video files. Dream Hub is for entertainment only and is not a gambling service.

Changes
